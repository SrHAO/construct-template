Technical datasheet for Construrama's **acrylic waterproof coating**.

- Applications: rooftops, flat and pitched slabs, terraces and exposed walls.
- Type: water-based acrylic, ready to use with reinforcing fiber.
- Coverage: approximately 1 liter per m² in two coats.
- Features: excellent adhesion, flexibility and UV resistance.
- Packaging: 19-liter pails and 4-liter cans.
