Technical datasheet for Construrama's **hydrated lime**.

- Applications: masonry mortars, plasters, stucco and lime paint.
- Type: hydrated lime type N, in accordance with NMX-C-036.
- Yield: high bonding power with cement and sand.
- Packaging: 25 kg bags.
- Storage: protected from moisture to preserve its reactivity.
