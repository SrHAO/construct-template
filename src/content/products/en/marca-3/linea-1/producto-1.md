Technical datasheet for Construrama's **concrete block**.

- Applications: load-bearing walls, partitions, facades and perimeter fences.
- Sizes: 12×20×40 cm, with two or three hollows and other modules.
- Strength: class A for load-bearing walls and class B for partition walls.
- Finish: smooth or grooved texture for plastering or leaving exposed.
- Packing: standard pallets for easy handling on site.
