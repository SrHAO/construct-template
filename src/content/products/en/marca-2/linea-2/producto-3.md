Technical datasheet for Cemex's **ready-mix concrete**.

- Applications: foundations, slabs, columns, prestressed elements and infrastructure works.
- Strength: from f'c 150 to f'c 500 kg/cm² per design.
- Options: pumpable, with normal or lightweight aggregate and admixtures.
- Control: plant batching with quality certificate per pour.
- Supply: mixer trucks with just-in-time delivery to the site.
