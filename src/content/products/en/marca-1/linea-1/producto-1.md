Technical datasheet for Ternium's **structural steel plate**.

- Applications: beams, columns, machine bases, welded connections and steel structures in general.
- Material: carbon steel grades A-36 and A-572, as required.
- Availability: thicknesses from 1/8" (3.2 mm); cut and sheared to size.
- Surface: clean and ready for welding or coating.
- Quality: certified product with mechanical property control.
