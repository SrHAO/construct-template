Technical datasheet for Ternium's **galvanized steel sheet**.

- Applications: roofs, walls, facades, ducts and elements exposed to weather.
- Coating: zinc applied by hot-dip galvanizing.
- Formats: cold- or hot-rolled, in coils, sheets or cut-to-length.
- Thickness: from 26 gauge to 12 gauge.
- Certification: meets coating and bending standards.
